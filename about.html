Our Story

Nairobi's Car Care & Cleaning Experts — at Your Door

Crystal Crown was founded with a simple but powerful idea: professional-grade car detailing and house cleaning should be accessible to everyone, without the inconvenience of leaving your home or office.

We started with a single mobile detailing unit in Nairobi and a commitment to using only eco-friendly, professional-grade products. Today, Crystal Crown serves over 20 neighbourhoods across the city, with a growing team of certified cleaning specialists.

Every job we take is personal. We treat your car or home with the same care and attention to detail we would give our own. That standard — and that mindset — is what defines Crystal Crown.

Purpose & Direction

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make professional car detailing and home cleaning effortlessly accessible to every Nairobi resident — delivered at their door, at fair prices, with zero compromise on quality or environmental responsibility.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading mobile cleaning service brand — recognised for reliability, eco-conscious practices, and a customer-first approach that consistently delivers results beyond expectations.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Six principles that guide every decision we make and every service we deliver.

Convenience

We come to you — no wasted trips, no queues, no disruption to your day. Scheduling is easy and we're always on time.

Eco-Friendly

We use biodegradable, water-efficient cleaning methods. Our steam technology uses up to 80% less water than traditional car washes.

Professional

Every Crystal Crown team member is trained, vetted, and equipped with professional-grade tools and products for superior results.

Affordable

Premium results at honest prices. No hidden fees — we confirm pricing before starting so there are never any surprises.

Trusted

Fully insured team, 4.9-star Google rating, and a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, we come back — free of charge.

Reliable

We show up on time, every time. Consistent quality is our standard — not just a promise.
