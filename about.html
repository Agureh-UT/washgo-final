How It All Started

Our Story

Crystal Crown was born out of frustration — and a vision. Our founder spent years watching people waste entire weekends driving to car washes, waiting in queues, and settling for mediocre results. Meanwhile, their homes sat in need of the same attention and care.

In 2021, we launched with a single-vehicle operation. The mission was clear: bring professional-grade detailing and home cleaning straight to the client. No waiting. No compromise. No fixed location needed.

Today, Crystal Crown operates across multiple cities in Kenya with a growing team of certified technicians, a fleet of fully-equipped mobile units, and a reputation built entirely on word-of-mouth from satisfied clients.

We are proudly mobile-first, and that's not changing. Because the best service is the one that fits seamlessly into your life — not around it.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Values

Everything we do at Crystal Crown comes back to three core commitments that define how we operate and why clients come back.

🌟

Excellence in Every Detail

We hold ourselves to the highest professional standard on every single job — whether it's a quick interior refresh or a full ceramic coating. No shortcuts, no compromise.

💧

Eco-Conscious Cleaning

We use biodegradable, low-water cleaning products that are safe for your family, pets, vehicles, and the planet. Professional results without environmental guilt.

🤝

Client-First Always

Your time, comfort, and satisfaction matter above all else. We communicate transparently, arrive on time, and back every job with our no-quibble guarantee.

Trust & Transparency

🚀

Continuous Improvement

We invest in training, equipment, and products to stay at the forefront of professional detailing and home cleaning. Our team never stops learning.

Community Commitment

We are proud to employ local talent, source locally where possible, and give back to the communities we operate in through skills training and sponsorship.
