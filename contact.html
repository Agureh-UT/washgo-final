Frequently Asked Questions

We bring our fully-equipped mobile unit directly to your home, office, or any location where your vehicle is parked. Our team carries all the equipment, water, and products needed — you don't need to provide anything.

We currently operate across Nairobi, Mombasa, and Eldoret. Contact us if you're in a nearby area — we may be able to accommodate. Travel fees may apply for locations beyond our standard coverage zone.

Service times vary by type: Quick detail: 1–1.5 hrs | Interior & Exterior: 2–3 hrs | Ceramic Coating: half to full day | Home Standard Clean: 2–3 hrs | Deep Clean: 4–6 hrs. We'll give you a precise estimate when you book.

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and cash on completion. No deposit is required for standard bookings — you pay only after the service is complete and you're satisfied.

We stand behind every job with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the result, contact us within 48 hours and we'll return to correct it — completely free of charge, no questions asked.
