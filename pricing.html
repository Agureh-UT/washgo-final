Pricing Questions

Common Pricing FAQs

The prices shown are base prices and are the most you'll pay for the standard service. Optional add-ons (air freshener, before/after photos, pet hair removal, etc.) are listed separately and only charged if you specifically request them at booking.

No travel charges within our standard service zones (20+ areas across Nairobi). For locations outside our standard zones, a small fuel surcharge may apply — we'll let you know upfront before confirming your booking.

We accept M-Pesa, cash, and bank transfer. Payment is made after the service is complete and you're satisfied. M-Pesa is the most convenient — our Paybill details will be shared with your confirmation.

Yes! Regular clients (weekly/bi-weekly) receive up to 15% off. Businesses booking 3+ vehicles or premises at once receive a 10% fleet discount. WhatsApp us to arrange a custom package.

We have a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, tell our technician immediately and we'll re-do the affected area at no extra charge. If still unsatisfied, we offer a partial or full refund depending on the circumstances.
